## Sudoku Solver 1.0.0

The third app in the puzzle suite, after Cube Solver and Chess Solver.

### Added
- A 9x9 grid you can fill by clicking, typing, the number pad or the digit buttons, with locked given digits, pencil marks (by hand or automatic), paint mode and red highlighting for clashes.
- New puzzles at Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert and Fiendish. Every one has exactly one answer, rated by the hardest trick it needs.
- Five puzzle shapes: Classic, Diagonal, Killer (cages with sums), Mini 6x6 and Mini 4x4.
- Show mistakes: wrong digits are marked straight away (you can switch it off in Settings).
- Hints that name the trick a person would use next, outline the squares involved and explain it in plain words.
- Step-by-step playback of the whole solution with a speed slider, and a clickable history to jump backwards or forwards.
- An instant solver that also spots puzzles with no answer or more than one.
- Type in your own puzzle, load one from its digits, or share a link with your progress.
- A timer, keyboard shortcuts, lessons on the rules and tricks, and an app tour.
- Four themes (Matte, Frutiger Aero, Neon, Neubrutalism), each light and dark, following your computer by default.
- Works offline in the browser, installs as a web app, and installs on Windows.
